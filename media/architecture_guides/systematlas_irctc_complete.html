01 / At a glance

The system, visually

A request comes from a customer, passes through a gateway and purpose-built services, then reaches the right data or external provider. Reads are optimized for speed; booking writes are carefully controlled to avoid selling the same berth twice.

01Separate reads from writes

Search can use caches and replicas. Booking must verify and change authoritative inventory.

02Protect scarce inventory

A berth must be reserved atomically for every segment in the requested journey.

03Recover from partial failure

Payments and notifications can fail independently, so use durable states, retries, and reconciliation.
